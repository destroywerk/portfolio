I've worked in product design for 14 years and graphic design for 5 before that. I've worked as a Principal Product Designer simplifying complex systems in HR SaaS, defined Material design for enterprise mobility, designed core elements of Pixel OS for 2+ billion people, created the wearables experience principles for Google, and rebuilt consumer products from the ground up.

Previously at Google for 9 years working across mobile, wearables and tablet to launch Android Enterprise, Pixel Watch and Pixel Tablet, and most recently at Personio solving complex organisational structures. I'm currently at Preply, working on language learning at scale.
